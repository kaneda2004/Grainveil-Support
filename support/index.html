Grainveil for iPhone

Need a hand?

Report a problem or request help through the Grainveil project’s issue tracker. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you expected to happen. Do not attach private photographs or other sensitive information.

Contact Grainveil Support

Permission help

If the camera or gallery is unavailable, open iPhone Settings, choose Grainveil, and review Camera and Photos access. Grainveil needs add-only Photos access to save captures and asks for library access only when you open its gallery.
