From Natural’s quiet rendering to Veil’s swirling highlights, choose the character that suits the scene. Keep the controls close and your attention on the photograph.

01 · Style

Shape the finished image.

Choose smooth Natural tone or punchier High contrast. Add a glow around bright detail and choose the grain for your finished photograph. The Style panel stays below or beside the viewfinder, leaving the image undimmed.

  • Natural and High contrast
  • Off, Normal, and High bloom
  • Off, Fine, and Rich grain in the developed photograph

02 · Frame

See beyond the photograph.

Compose in 3:4, square, 2:1, or 3:1. An optional dimmed live area shows what sits just outside the crop, while the rule-of-thirds grid keeps the frame disciplined.

  • Classic and panoramic aspect ratios
  • Outside-frame live preview
  • Portrait and landscape layouts with an optional thirds grid

03 · Depth

Choose what falls away.

In Portrait, move Depth toward Shallow for more blur or toward Deep for less. Each depth-enabled lens remembers its own setting, so a subtle Natural portrait and a more expressive Veil photograph can both feel right.

  • Tap to choose your focus point
  • Clean, Natural, and Hex included
  • Dream, Veil, and Meridian in optional Collection I
  • Depth processing stays on your iPhone

Find your optical character

Three lenses to begin.
Three more to explore.

Clean, Natural, and Hex are included free. Collection I adds Dream’s soft glow, Veil’s swirling highlights, and Meridian’s smooth portrait rendering. Compare the lenses on the same scene before choosing.

Collection I is a one-time purchase with Family Sharing. No subscription. Restore your purchase in Settings → Lens Collections.
